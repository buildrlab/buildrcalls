Recording and permissions

Allow screen recording in macOS settings, then run a short test. Your first display is recorded, so keep the call and its chat visible on that screen. Your default browser can use your existing signed-in session. The app cannot verify admission or bypass a waiting room, login prompt, or CAPTCHA.

System audio support varies by macOS and Electron version. Play audio during the test and listen back before relying on a scheduled call.
